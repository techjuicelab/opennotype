## Downloaded models

Model files are downloaded separately when the user prepares the corresponding feature. They are not included in the source repository or the ordinary app bundle. The SDK pins do not currently pin a model repository revision or verify a project-maintained model hash; model distribution remains an upstream dependency.

### Whisper Core ML models

- Source and attribution: OpenAI Whisper models, converted/distributed for Core ML by Argmax through [argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml](https://huggingface.co/argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml).
- The upstream model card labels these artifacts **MIT**. This is separate from the WhisperKit SDK's MIT source license and its embedded third-party notices.
- The default variant is `openai_whisper-large-v3-v20240930_626MB`. Additional variants represented in the local engine are `openai_whisper-small` and `openai_whisper-base`; they are not bundled downloads.
- OpenNoType selects and loads these artifacts without modifying their model weights.

### Speaker diarization and embedding models

- Source and Core ML conversion: [FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml).
- The model card identifies **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** and distinguishes that model license from the FluidAudio SDK's Apache-2.0 license. License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [legal text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
- Artifacts selected by the application are `pyannote_segmentation.mlmodelc` and `wespeaker_v2.mlmodelc`.
- Segmentation attribution: Alexis Plaquet and Hervé Bredin, *Powerset multi-class cross entropy loss for neural speaker diarization*, INTERSPEECH 2023.
- Speaker-embedding attribution: Hongji Wang, Chengdong Liang, Shuai Wang, Zhengyang Chen, Binbin Zhang, Xu Xiang, Yanlei Deng, and Yanmin Qian, *Wespeaker: A research and production oriented speaker embedding learning toolkit*, ICASSP 2023.
- The upstream card also credits Federico Landini, Ján Profant, Mireia Diez, and Lukáš Burget for *Bayesian HMM clustering of x-vector sequences (VBx) in speaker diarization: theory, implementation and analysis on standard tasks*, Computer Speech & Language, 2022.
- OpenNoType uses the distributed Core ML weights without modifying them and applies its own provisional segment-selection policy around their output. No upstream author or distributor endorses this application.
